Tools that help you record what matters and reflect on what changes.

Your migraine journal

Record episodes, symptoms, triggers and medication. Look back at your own history and everyday patterns.

↗

Movement & recovery

Read shared steps, heart-rate measurements, sleep and exercise through Android Health Connect, when permission and records are available.

≈

Food & hydration

Keep water and meal logs, set personal goals and request optional AI nutrition estimates from descriptions or an explicitly shared photo.

♡

Optional AI coaching

Explore wellness and fitness questions with AI personas using your own provider keys. Choose whether to include your health summary in each request.

☁

Your account, your backup

Opt in to your Google Drive app-data backup to restore your profile, journal, settings and AI keys when you return.

◎

Privacy you can understand

Separate account storage, phone-controlled health permissions and clear choices for cloud features. No advertising or sale of health data.

FROM YOUR DEVICES TO YOUR DAY

Connected through
Health Connect.

Samsung Health and other compatible apps can share records with Android Health Connect. MigraineGuard reads the records you allow on your phone.

Sync depends on the source app, available record types and timing. This is not a live watch connection; matching a watch display is not guaranteed. Fitbit support depends on its Health Connect sharing.
