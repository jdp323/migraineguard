A BIGGER PICTURE, WITHOUT THE CLUTTER

Built around real life.

Tools that help you record what matters and reflect on what changes.

✦

Your migraine journal

Record episodes, symptoms, triggers and medication. Look back at your own history and everyday patterns.

↗

Movement & recovery

Select muscles on an anatomical front/back map, build editable workouts and review a weekly training plan. Read compatible Health Connect data and set movement reminders. Log actual sets, effort, treadmill details and holds; track your own progress.

≈

Food & hydration

Keep water and meal logs, set personal goals and request optional AI nutrition estimates from descriptions or an explicitly shared photo.

♡

Optional AI coaching

Explore wellness and fitness questions with AI personas using your own provider keys. Choose whether to include your health summary in each request.

☁

Your account, your backup

Opt in to your Google Drive app-data backup to restore your profile, journal, settings and AI keys when you return.

◎

Privacy you can understand

Keep private body-progress photos and comparisons on your phone, with a separate Drive inclusion choice. Export your records from Settings → Data. Separate account storage, phone-controlled health permissions and clear choices for cloud features. No advertising or sale of health data.

FROM YOUR DEVICES TO YOUR DAY

Connected through
Health Connect.

Samsung Health, Garmin Connect, Fitbit / Google Health and other compatible apps can share records with Android Health Connect. MigraineGuard reads the records you allow on your phone.

Sync depends on the source app, available record types and timing. This is not a live watch connection; matching a watch display is not guaranteed. Each source must share the record types you need.
